A professional voice-over doesn’t happen by accident.

That’s why we follow a 5 step process, just like a real professional sound studio.

Five steps.

Not four.

Not “almost five”.

Just five.

Let’s start with step one.

Step 1.

We prepare the script

We give your script a polish so it doesn’t sound like shampoo instructions. Clear, natural phrasing and, when needed, a few tricks so the AI pronounces things exactly right.

Step 2.

We choose the right voice

According to the type of project (audiobook, ad, online course…), we choose together the voice that best conveys your message. Because it’s not the same to narrate a meditation audiobook as it is to voice a burger commercial...
Discover your ideal voice

Step 3.

We manage the rights

We also take care of the boring part the paperwork so everything is legally covered. Contracts with actors, licensing agreements…
Whatever you need to sleep peacefully at night.

Step 4.

We generate the voice over

We fine tune the intonation, pacing and style until it sounds like a real voice over. We clean up clicks, pops and mouth noises; ensure tonal consistency throughout the narration, and level the audio to meet the standards of wherever you plan to publish it.

Step 5.

We review the project and deliver it

We listen with a critical ear: if something sounds off, we fix it. And when it’s spot on, we package it nicely and deliver it in the format you need (WAV, MP3 or FLAC).
Done! Ribbon on top now go keep being awesome.

How DigiTalks works?


A little black magic, powered by AI. We fine-tune the script, tweak the right knobs, and deliver a professional voice over ready to use. If you want the details, just ask us.

Are you sure it doesn’t sound like a hungover robot?

We’re confident. And so are our clients. But you shouldn’t take our word for it, you don’t know us yet. So see (and hear) for yourself: leave your email and listen to real examples.

Are all the voices you use exclusive to DigiTalks?


It depends, and it depends on how much you’re willing to invest.
Exclusive voices come with an additional fee that goes directly to the voice owner. We earn the same either way.

If your budget is tight, we can also work with catalog voices and apply our expertise so they sound far better than anything you’d achieve on your own.

What languages are available?

Quite a few. From neutral Spanish to Catalan or Latin American Spanish, all the way to “Wisconsin” English.
You tell us where you want your message to land, and we’ll give it the right voice.

Do you have permission to use those voices?
