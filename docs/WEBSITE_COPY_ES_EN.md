### From script to delivery.



The whole production or the stage you need. We agree the materials, voices and deliverables before work begins.

- **Translation and adaptation**: Text, context, character and timing.

- **Casting and voices**: Selection, creation or generation to fit the project.

- **Studio and AI dubbing**: Directed performance and voice production.

- **Post-production and mixing**: Editing, synchronization and audio finishing.

- **Review and quality control**: Linguistic review and technical checks.

- **Subtitling**: Translation and timing where the project requires it.

### Different characters. Different voices.



Actors, AI voices or both. We choose the method around your character, content and budget. Creating the voice and producing the dub are separate stages.

- **Professional actors**: Casting, direction and studio performance.

- **Custom AI voices**: Voice creation for the project’s requirements, with the appropriate permissions.

- **Synthetic and catalogue voices**: Designed or selected options with their own usage terms; catalogue voices are non-exclusive.

- **Hybrid production**: Combine human performance and generated voices within one production.

Voice rights, consent, permitted uses and any exclusivity are agreed per project.

### Less back-and-forth. A clear process.





- **Materials**: Scripts, audio and references.

- **Adaptation**: Scripts prepared for the language and character.

- **Voices**: Casting and production method.

- **Production**: Dubbing, editing and mixing.

- **Review and delivery**: Linguistic and technical review, then final assets.
